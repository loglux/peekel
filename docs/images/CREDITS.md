# Credits of the pictures

The screens (`far-*.png`) are what a real Far Manager with the Peekel plugin wrote into a pseudo-terminal, drawn by a script of the
development repository (they are not photographs of a screen). The documents in them are invented.

The photograph in `far-quickview.png` (a brown bear at Naknek Lake, Brooks Camp, Katmai National Park, Alaska) is
"Brown bear Brooks Camp.jpg" by Roy Wood, National Park Service, in the **public domain** (a work of an employee of the United
States federal government): <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brown_bear_Brooks_Camp.jpg>. The original is not in this
repository; the picture shows it scaled down.

The PDF in `far-pdf.png` is the cover of the "SLS Brochure" (Space Launch System, 5 February 2020) of the National
Aeronautics and Space Administration, a work of the United States federal government, marked **public domain** on Wikimedia
Commons: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SLS_Brochure_02-05-20_(656631541636).pdf>. The cover shows the core stage
for Artemis I in the B-2 test stand at Stennis Space Center (a NASA photograph) and the NASA insignia; Peekel and its authors
are not connected with NASA and are not endorsed by it. The original is not in this repository.

`far-recovered.png`: a copy of an invented Word document, renamed like a fragment of a disk check.
